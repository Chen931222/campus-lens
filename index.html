Campus Lens

English margin notes for Chinese-only university admin pages. The page is read by the AI in your own browser; the cloud only ever sees a copy with your personal data removed.

Try it on a course-withdrawal page Code and Chrome extension

What it does

  • Explain this page in English. A short note beside every field: what it is and the one rule that matters.
  • Ask about the page. Answers come from the school's rules, with the rule cited.
  • Can I withdraw these? Tick courses. Credit limits and course limits are checked by code, not by the AI, and a "no" says which smaller choice is allowed.

Who does what

Your page (ID, name, courses, phone)Gemini Nano on your computer, through Firebase AI Logic. Nothing on the page leaves your browser.
No on-device modelThe cloud model, given a copy with your personal values blanked first.
The school's public regulationsTurned into English rules by the cloud model once, checked by code, approved by a person.
Limits and "can I withdraw"Code. The small model got the arithmetic wrong in testing.

Limits

One fictional school and one page; every rule is invented for the demo. The rules were reviewed by the developer, not school staff. No international student has tested it yet.
